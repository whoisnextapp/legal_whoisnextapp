## 2. Our approach to privacy (design principles)

By the nature of a "social matching/dating" platform, we design the Platform without concealing the fact that some information will be visible, while aiming to provide the user with a **control space**.

Our core principles:

- **Purpose limitation**: We process data for purposes that are genuinely necessary to provide the Platform to you.
- **Data minimization**: We aim not to open unnecessary data fields and, if opened, not to retain data unnecessarily.
- **Layered transparency**: We build bridges between documents for users who want detail (KVKK, Cookie, Explicit Consent, Biometric).
- **User control**: We base our approach on enabling you to manage permissions and consents (and withdraw them).
- **Security and abuse prevention**: We focus on reducing risks of fake accounts, spam, harassment, and fraud.

## 4. What information do we collect? (details by WIN flows)

In this section, we explain "which data is generated at which step" based on typical Platform flows. For the legal framework and data categories table, the [KVKK Information Notice](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/kvkk-aydinlatma-metni) should also be reviewed.

### 4.1 Account creation and login (Google / Apple / phone)

Login to WIN may be provided via **Sign in with Google (OAuth)**, **Sign in with Apple** (iOS devices only) or **phone number login** (SMS/OTP).

**Sign in with Google:** Google transmits the information it states will be shared from your account with the Platform. Typically, name (or Google profile name), e-mail address, profile photo, and Google user ID (technical) may be received. The e-mail address is generally considered **verified** in line with Google processes. After profile creation, **phone number verification** may be required for certain interactions such as making picks and sending messages; until this verification is completed, related functions may be restricted.

**Sign in with Apple (iOS):** Apple transmits the information you choose to share during sign-in: a technical user identifier that Apple creates for your account, an e-mail address (if you use Apple's "Hide My Email" option, a relay address generated by Apple that forwards to your real address) and, if you choose to share it, your name (Apple generally provides this only at the first sign-in). With Sign in with Apple, too, **phone number verification** may be required for interactions such as making picks and sending messages.

**Phone login:** Identity verification is OTP-based. **E-mail verification** and **phone number verification** steps are positioned to be completed **during the profile creation process** rather than at the moment of registration.

<Callout type="info" title="Third-party login (OAuth) note">
Google and Apple act as **independent data controllers** for their own services. The Platform uses information received from Google or Apple to carry out account creation and login processes. In SMS/OTP verification, communication providers (e.g., Twilio) may be involved as technical processors. For detailed role and transfer explanations, the [KVKK Information Notice](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/kvkk-aydinlatma-metni) and the relevant third parties' own policies apply.
</Callout>

### 4.4 "Value & compatibility" parameters (sensitive field risk)

In WIN, there may be some "importance level" questions to increase matching compatibility. Example:

- "**How important is religion in your life?**" (Options: I do not believe, Not central in my life, Important, Very important)
- "**How important is the political view of the person you will match with for you?**" (Options: I am not interested in politics, They may have a different view, Important to some extent, It is very important that we share the same view)
- "How important are appearance / financial status?"

Some of these questions may involve processing data that is **special category personal data** or may create **inferences** in that direction. Therefore:

- an **additional information and explicit consent** approach is adopted for relevant fields,
- the highest possible privacy and security standards are applied,
- we aim to enable you to manage your preferences for these fields.

For the legal framework: [KVKK Information Notice](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/kvkk-aydinlatma-metni) and [Explicit Consent Texts](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/acik-riza-metinleri).

Your profile typically contains **6 photo slots**. In order to maintain safety on the Platform, uploading at least one photo where **your face is clearly visible** and completing the selfie verification step is **required as a rule.**

**Selfie Verification Flow:**
- Instant capture is made only via the **front camera**; **photos cannot be uploaded from the gallery.**
- We perform pose and profile photo consistency checks with **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash**; and inappropriate content checks on the selfie with **safeSearchDetection (Google Cloud Vision)**.
- This step is a functional requirement to prevent fake accounts; **as a rule**, if consent is not given or at least one photo is not verified, the account is not included in the discovery flow (Deck). WIN may, by administrator decision and in limited cases, grant an exception to this rule for certain accounts; in that case no verification badge appears on unverified photos. It is therefore not guaranteed that every profile in the discovery flow is verified; you can see whether a photo is verified from the badge on it.
- A green checkmark/badge is placed on each verified photo. Verification is not performed on photos with other people; the verification badge appears only on photos where only the user is present.
- During photo upload:

- gallery/camera access permissions,
- photo files and technical metadata (e.g., file size, format)

may be included.

> Note: It is intended not to perform biometric inference from your photos; however, features such as selfie verification are managed with separate texts as they may create an additional biometric assessment risk.

### 4.5 Birth details and astrology features

During profile creation you are asked for your **date of birth** together with your **place of birth** (country / province / district) and your **time of birth**. The place of birth must be filled in at this step; if you do not know your time of birth you can tick "I don't know" (in that case "unknown" is stored instead of a time).

- **What is processed?** The name of the place of birth; its approximate coordinates (at settlement level) and time zone; the time of birth or the "unknown" flag; and the zodiac sign and natal chart results calculated from these.
- **How?** The coordinates of your place of birth are obtained through your device operating system's address resolution (geocoding) service (Apple on iOS, Google services on Android). Natal chart and astrological compatibility calculations run on WIN's cloud infrastructure (Google Cloud).
- **Why?** To show your zodiac sign and to provide natal chart and astrological compatibility analyses (detailed analyses are part of WIN Premium).
- **Visibility:** Your place and time of birth are not shown to other users on the profile screen; other users may see your zodiac sign and (where offered) the astrological compatibility result between you and them. However, so that astrological compatibility can be calculated, when your profile appears in another user's deck your date of birth, time of birth and place of birth (including the place name and its approximate coordinates) are technically transmitted to that user's app; this information is not displayed on that user's screen.
- **Legal basis:** Provision of the astrology features (performance of the contract, KVKK Art. 5/2-c). Details: [KVKK Information Notice](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/kvkk-aydinlatma-metni).

Selfie/liveness verification may be applied to reduce fake accounts and increase user trust. In this flow **camera permission** may be requested.

For how voice messages are processed and retained, see Section 4.9.

The selfie verification images are intended not to be displayed on the profile; for the processing, retention, and destruction approach in this area: [Biometric Data Notice and Consent](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/biyometrik-veri-aydinlatma-riza).

### 4.8 Face data — collection, use, sharing, and retention (single summary)

In line with our Platform transparency commitment, we summarize in this Privacy Policy what is meant by **face data**. For the detailed legal framework, consent text, and provider tables: [Biometric Data Notice and Consent](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/biyometrik-veri-aydinlatma-riza).

**What is collected?** In the verification flow: a **selfie image** captured instantly with the **front camera** (gallery upload is not provided); requested **pose/liveness** verification; **technical outputs** produced within **face verification** performed between the selfie and your **profile photos** (e.g., similarity score, match/verification result, status information); limited **processing metadata** (e.g., verification time, number of attempts, error code); and **security logs** for security and objection processes. General photo uploads published on the profile and verification selfie are designed separately; it is intended that the verification selfie is not shown on the profile.

**For what purposes is it used?** To increase the likelihood that the profile belongs to a real person, reduce **fake accounts** and abuse, protect Platform security and service integrity; to conduct reviews in verification objections; and to comply with legal obligations and protect rights. Face/biometric-type data processed in this scope is not used for **ad targeting, marketing profiling, or campaign personalization**.

**Third parties and storage location** The verification process may be used for limited automated evaluation with **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash** (pose and profile consistency / person verification) and **Google Cloud Vision** (**safeSearchDetection** — inappropriate content control). As infrastructure, hosting and technical logs may be involved on the **Google Cloud / Firebase** side. Retention of a permanent **biometric template** or **embedding** is **not intended**. Provider roles and location/transfer details are explained in [Biometric Data Notice and Consent](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/biyometrik-veri-aydinlatma-riza) and [KVKK Information Notice](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/kvkk-aydinlatma-metni).

**Retention period** Data in the nature of a **biometric map** subject to face recognition processing is **immediately destroyed** after verification is completed. In the system, a non-biometric status such as **"verified: yes/no"** may be kept. **Non-biometric** audit/technical metadata records kept for process security may be retained for a limited period within legal limitation and legitimate interest periods. Consent withdrawal and exceptional long-retention cases: [Biometric Data Notice and Consent](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/biyometrik-veri-aydinlatma-riza) **Section 9-10**.

Other related sections: **4.2** (consent layer), **4.4** (profile photos and verification flow summary), **4.7** (permissions and microphone note).

Audio files are stored on Google Cloud / Firebase Storage; within the app only the two parties to the chat can access them and, like text messages, they may be reviewed by authorised admins/moderators for moderation and security purposes. As a rule, voice messages are deleted automatically **30 days after** they are sent; for copies placed in the legal archive when an account is deleted, see Section 9. No voice recognition or biometric analysis is performed on your recordings.
- **Security signals:** Complaint, blocking, and suspicious behavior records.

This data may be used to operate the matching experience, prevent abuse, and manage disputes.

### 4.13 Single-device session and installation identifier

As a rule, your WIN account can be signed in on **one device at a time**. To ensure this, the app uses an **installation/device identifier** for each installation and stores it on your account:

- **Android:** A random installation identifier generated by the app on first launch (it starts with "wn-").
- **iOS:** The identifier Apple provides per device for each app developer (IDFV).

When you sign in from another device, your account moves to that device and the session on the previous device is closed. The same identifier and your IP address are also used in device- and IP-based sending limits to prevent abuse of SMS verification codes. On Android, to detect whether the identifier was carried over to another phone from a backup, a signature made of the device manufacturer/brand/model is kept only on your device; it is not sent to the server (on iOS this check uses the IDFV itself, which is also stored on the account). WIN uses this identifier for the single-device session and account security (on iOS the same IDFV value may also be collected by our subscription infrastructure provider; see Section 4.14). Legal basis: performance of the contract and legitimate interest (account and service security).

## 6. Matching/recommendation system and "profiling"

In the matching and recommendation (deck) flow, WIN evaluates certain parameters together to produce profile ranking/compatibility. This process may in some cases be considered **profiling**.

### 6.1 Main signals used (example)

- **Profile information**: age, gender, height, sign, profession, etc.
- **Preference/importance parameters**: choices such as appearance/financial status/political view compatibility
- **Location/distance**: proximity
- **Usage behaviors**: like/dislike, matching patterns (within the Platform)
- **Verification signals**: profile verification status and badges
- **Cooldown**: Rule that profiles shown to you in the deck flow but not selected are not shown again for a certain period
- **Newness and activity**: additional visibility given to newly joined profiles in their first days; whether a profile has been active recently (profiles that have not signed in for a long time are shown much less)
- **Profile score**: a technical score based on how complete the profile is
- **Editorial selection and moderation**: profiles highlighted by the WIN team for a limited time; accounts whose visibility is reduced for a limited time or that are removed from the discovery flow by moderation decision (details: [DSA Compliance Page](/kurumsal-ve-yasal-uyumluluk/dsa-uyum-sayfasi) Section 12)
- **Premium effects**: 12 decks every 12 hours, additional visibility weight in decks, priority access to newly joined profiles, appearing near the top of the picked person's list, deck filtering, profile hiding, online/offline status viewing, score viewing; the one-time Visibility Week purchase also adds visibility weight in decks while it is active (it does not grant choosing)

<Callout type="info" title="Control is with you: what can you do?">
- You can update your preferences and manage your permissions.  
- Under Article 11 of KVKK, if you believe an unfavorable result has arisen exclusively from automated analysis, you may object (see [KVKK Information Notice](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/kvkk-aydinlatma-metni)).
</Callout>

### 6.2 Discrimination and sensitive areas

Fields such as religion/political view require special attention both in terms of sensitive data nature and discrimination risk. Therefore:

- **explicit consent and transparency** are taken as basis in these areas,
- compatibility is targeted with the minimum data possible,
- users are intended to be able to manage their preferences.

If services are provided in the EU, an additional bridge may be established with the [DSA Compliance Page](/kurumsal-ve-yasal-uyumluluk/dsa-uyum-sayfasi) in terms of recommendation system transparency.

## 9. How long do we retain data? (logic)

The retention period of data varies according to processing purpose, legal obligations, and dispute/security needs.

Example:

- **While your account is active**: data required to provide the service is retained.
- **Voice messages**: as a rule, deleted automatically 30 days after they are sent; no archive copy is made in this deletion.
- **When your account is deleted**: your profile, photos, verification selfies, support attachments and sign-in record are deleted and your profile is closed to other users. However, **not all of your data is deleted immediately**; the following records are retained for the periods stated:
  - **Copy of the deleted account:** At the moment of deletion, a full copy of your profile record (excluding notification tokens) and the deletion reason you selected are retained for **90 days**, with access limited to authorised staff, and are deleted automatically at the end of that period. For copies of accounts deleted before this retention rule began to be applied in September 2026, the 90-day period runs from the date the rule began to be applied. Purpose: abuse and fraud investigations, possible disputes and requests from authorities.
  - **Chat archive:** During account deletion your chats are not deleted without being archived. When an account is deleted, the messages between you and the other party and the voice message files whose automatic 30-day deletion period has not yet expired are closed to other users and moved to a legal archive with access limited to authorised staff; this archive is retained for the relevant limitation periods for disputes and requests from authorities.
  - **Banned accounts:** If an account whose access was blocked for violating the Community Guidelines is deleted, the ban record of that account (WIN user ID, phone number, e-mail address where available and the reason for the ban) is retained **without a time limit** unless the ban is lifted, to prevent the banned person from opening a new account.
  - **Other records:** log/traffic records, moderation decisions and evidence of violations, and commercial/transaction records are retained for the periods in the legislation and in Section 9 of the [KVKK Information Notice](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/kvkk-aydinlatma-metni). Records held by third-party providers (e.g. subscription infrastructure, analytics and advertising measurement) are subject to the retention periods of the relevant provider.

For the detailed retention approach: [KVKK Information Notice](/gizlilik-ve-veri-koruma/kvkk-aydinlatma-metni) and [Terms of Use — Section 13](/sozlesmeler/kullanim-kosullari#bölüm-13-hesap-sonlandırma-ve-fesih).
